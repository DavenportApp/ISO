# 4.3 Determining the Scope of the Quality Management System

## Requirement

The organization shall determine the boundaries and applicability of the QMS to establish its scope. When determining the scope, the organization shall consider:
- External and internal issues (4.1)
- Requirements of interested parties (4.2)
- Products and services of the organization

The scope shall be available as documented information and include products/services covered, and justification for any exclusions.

## Scope Determination Process

### Step 1: Context Analysis Review
Review outputs from:
- 4.1 Context analysis (internal/external issues)
- 4.2 Interested parties and their requirements
- Strategic direction and objectives
- Climate impact assessment
- Digital maturity assessment

### Step 2: Process Mapping
- Identify all organizational processes
- Determine process ownership
- Map process interactions
- Identify processes affecting quality
- Include digital and sustainability processes

### Step 3: Product/Service Analysis
- List all products and services
- Identify quality-critical characteristics
- Determine customer requirements
- Assess regulatory requirements
- Evaluate sustainability aspects

### Step 4: Boundary Definition
- Define geographical boundaries
- Determine organizational boundaries
- Identify functional boundaries
- Establish digital system boundaries
- Define supply chain boundaries

### Step 5: Exclusion Evaluation
- Review ISO 9001:2026 requirements
- Justify any exclusions
- Ensure exclusions don't affect conformity
- Document rationale
- Obtain top management approval

### Step 6: Scope Documentation
- Draft scope statement
- Review with stakeholders
- Obtain management approval
- Communicate to organization
- Make available to interested parties

## ISO 9001:2026 Scope Considerations

### Climate Change Integration
**Scope Must Address:**
- Climate-related risks affecting operations
- Environmental aspects of products/services
- Carbon footprint within boundaries
- Climate adaptation measures
- Sustainability goals and targets

### Digital Transformation
**Scope Must Include:**
- Digital quality management tools
- Data management systems
- Cybersecurity controls
- Digital customer interfaces
- Automation and AI systems

### Sustainability Practices
**Scope Coverage:**
- Environmental management aspects
- Social responsibility considerations
- Ethical sourcing practices
- Circular economy initiatives
- Sustainable development goals alignment

## Scope Review and Update

### Review Triggers
- Annual management review
- Significant organizational changes
- New products or services
- Market expansion or contraction
- Regulatory changes
- Climate risk reassessment
- Digital transformation milestones
- Merger or acquisition
- Technology changes

### Review Process
1. Gather inputs from context and stakeholder analysis
2. Assess continued appropriateness
3. Evaluate potential changes
4. Analyze impact of changes
5. Update scope statement if needed
6. Obtain management approval
7. Communicate changes
8. Update related documentation

### Change Management
- Document reason for change
- Assess impact on QMS
- Update affected processes
- Retrain personnel
- Update external communications
- Notify certification body (if certified)
