# 4.4 Quality Management System and Its Processes

## Requirement

The organization shall establish, implement, maintain and continually improve a quality management system, including the processes needed and their interactions, in accordance with ISO 9001:2026 requirements.

## Process Approach

The QMS is based on the process approach, which incorporates the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle and risk-based thinking.

### PDCA Cycle Application

**Plan:**
- Establish objectives and processes
- Identify risks and opportunities
- Plan actions to address them

**Do:**
- Implement planned processes
- Execute operational activities
- Provide resources and support

**Check:**
- Monitor and measure processes
- Analyze data and information
- Evaluate performance

**Act:**
- Take corrective actions
- Implement improvements
- Update processes

## QMS Process Categories

### 1. Management Processes

#### Strategic Planning
- **Purpose:** Define organizational direction and QMS strategy
- **Inputs:** Context analysis, stakeholder requirements, performance data
- **Activities:** Vision/mission development, strategic objectives, resource allocation
- **Outputs:** Strategic plan, quality policy, quality objectives
- **Owner:** Top Management
- **KPIs:** Strategy implementation rate, objective achievement

#### Management Review
- **Purpose:** Ensure QMS suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness
- **Inputs:** Audit results, performance data, customer feedback, risks/opportunities
- **Activities:** Review inputs, assess performance, make decisions
- **Outputs:** Improvement decisions, resource allocation, QMS changes
- **Owner:** Top Management
- **KPIs:** Action item completion rate, improvement initiatives launched

#### Risk and Opportunity Management
- **Purpose:** Address risks and opportunities affecting QMS
- **Inputs:** Context issues, stakeholder requirements, process data
- **Activities:** Risk identification, assessment, treatment, monitoring
- **Outputs:** Risk register, mitigation plans, opportunity actions
- **Owner:** Quality Manager
- **KPIs:** Risk mitigation effectiveness, opportunity realization rate

#### Climate Change Management (ISO 9001:2026)
- **Purpose:** Address climate-related risks and opportunities
- **Inputs:** Climate data, regulations, stakeholder expectations
- **Activities:** Climate risk assessment, adaptation planning, mitigation actions
- **Outputs:** Climate action plan, carbon reduction initiatives, resilience measures
- **Owner:** Sustainability Manager
- **KPIs:** Carbon footprint reduction, climate resilience score

### 2. Core Processes (Operation)

#### Customer Relationship Management
- **Purpose:** Understand and fulfill customer requirements
- **Inputs:** Customer inquiries, market research, feedback
- **Activities:** Requirement determination, communication, relationship building
- **Outputs:** Customer requirements, contracts, satisfaction data
- **Owner:** Sales/Customer Service Manager
- **KPIs:** Customer satisfaction, retention rate, NPS score

#### Design and Development
- **Purpose:** Develop products/services meeting requirements
- **Inputs:** Customer requirements, regulations, technology capabilities
- **Activities:** Planning, design, verification, validation, changes
- **Outputs:** Design specifications, prototypes, validated designs
- **Owner:** R&D Manager
- **KPIs:** Design success rate, time-to-market, innovation index

#### Procurement and Supplier Management
- **Purpose:** Ensure purchased products/services meet requirements
- **Inputs:** Purchase requirements, supplier capabilities
- **Activities:** Supplier selection, evaluation, purchase, monitoring
- **Outputs:** Qualified suppliers, purchased items, supplier performance data
- **Owner:** Procurement Manager
- **KPIs:** Supplier quality rating, on-time delivery, cost savings

#### Production and Service Provision
- **Purpose:** Produce/deliver products and services
- **Inputs:** Designs, materials, customer orders, resources
- **Activities:** Production planning, execution, control, delivery
- **Outputs:** Finished products/services, delivery records
- **Owner:** Operations Manager
- **KPIs:** Production efficiency, first-pass yield, on-time delivery

#### Digital Operations (ISO 9001:2026)
- **Purpose:** Leverage digital tools for quality management
- **Inputs:** Quality data, digital infrastructure, user requirements
- **Activities:** Digital process automation, data analytics, system integration
- **Outputs:** Digital insights, automated workflows, real-time monitoring
- **Owner:** Digital Transformation Officer
- **KPIs:** Digitalization coverage, data accuracy, automation rate

### 3. Support Processes

#### Human Resource Management
- **Purpose:** Provide competent personnel for QMS
- **Inputs:** Competence requirements, workforce planning
- **Activities:** Recruitment, training, development, performance management
- **Outputs:** Competent staff, training records, performance evaluations
- **Owner:** HR Manager
- **KPIs:** Training effectiveness, employee competence level, retention

#### Infrastructure and Work Environment
- **Purpose:** Provide and maintain necessary facilities
- **Inputs:** Process requirements, regulations, technology needs
- **Activities:** Facility management, equipment maintenance, environment control
- **Outputs:** Operational facilities, maintenance records, safe environment
- **Owner:** Facilities Manager
- **KPIs:** Equipment uptime, maintenance effectiveness, safety incidents

#### Information Technology
- **Purpose:** Provide IT infrastructure and support for QMS
- **Inputs:** IT requirements, security needs, user needs
- **Activities:** System management, cybersecurity, data backup, support
- **Outputs:** Available systems, secure data, IT services
- **Owner:** IT Manager
- **KPIs:** System uptime, security incidents, user satisfaction

#### Document and Records Management
- **Purpose:** Control documented information
- **Inputs:** QMS documents, records, change requests
- **Activities:** Document creation, review, approval, distribution, archival
- **Outputs:** Controlled documents, maintained records, version history
- **Owner:** Quality Manager
- **KPIs:** Document accuracy, revision cycle time, accessibility

### 4. Measurement and Improvement Processes

#### Monitoring and Measurement
- **Purpose:** Evaluate QMS and process performance
- **Inputs:** Process data, product/service characteristics, customer feedback
- **Activities:** Data collection, analysis, reporting, trending
- **Outputs:** Performance reports, dashboards, trend analysis
- **Owner:** Quality Manager
- **KPIs:** Data completeness, reporting timeliness, metric achievement

#### Internal Audit
- **Purpose:** Ensure QMS conformity and effectiveness
- **Inputs:** Audit program, previous results, risk assessment
- **Activities:** Audit planning, execution, reporting, follow-up
- **Outputs:** Audit reports, findings, improvement opportunities
- **Owner:** Lead Auditor
- **KPIs:** Audit completion rate, finding closure time, conformance level

#### Corrective Action and Improvement
- **Purpose:** Eliminate nonconformities and drive improvement
- **Inputs:** Nonconformities, audit findings, performance gaps
- **Activities:** Root cause analysis, action planning, implementation, verification
- **Outputs:** Corrected processes, improvements, lesson learned
- **Owner:** Quality Manager
- **KPIs:** Corrective action effectiveness, recurrence rate, improvement rate

## Process Interaction and Sequence

### Process Map Overview

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    MANAGEMENT PROCESSES                      │
│  Strategic Planning | Management Review | Risk Management   │
│              Climate Change Management (NEW)                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                              ↓
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      CORE PROCESSES                          │
│  Customer Relations → Design & Development → Procurement →  │
│  Production & Service Provision → Digital Operations (NEW)  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                              ↑
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    SUPPORT PROCESSES                         │
│  HR | Infrastructure | IT | Document Management             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                              ↑
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              MEASUREMENT & IMPROVEMENT PROCESSES             │
│  Monitoring | Internal Audit | Corrective Action            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Key Process Interactions

1. **Strategic Planning → All Processes:** Provides direction and objectives
2. **Customer Relations → Design:** Customer requirements drive design inputs
3. **Design → Procurement:** Design specifications drive purchasing requirements
4. **Procurement → Production:** Materials and services enable production
5. **Production → Customer:** Delivery of products/services
6. **Digital Operations → All Processes:** Digital tools support all activities
7. **Monitoring → Management Review:** Performance data informs decisions
8. **Management Review → Improvement:** Decisions drive improvement actions
9. **Risk Management → All Processes:** Risk considerations integrated throughout

3. Plan change implementation

5. Implement change

7. Update documentation
8. Communicate changes
