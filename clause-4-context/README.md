# Clause 4: Context of the Organization

## Overview

This section establishes the foundation of our Quality Management System by defining the organizational context, interested parties, scope, and process approach.

- [4.1 Understanding the Organization and Its Context](./4.1-understanding-context.md)

## Purpose

Clause 4 ensures that:
- The organization understands its internal and external environment
- Relevant interested parties and their requirements are identified
- The QMS scope is clearly defined
- Key processes and their interactions are documented

## Key Requirements

✅ Identify internal and external issues affecting the QMS  
✅ Determine interested parties and their requirements  
✅ Define and document the QMS scope  
✅ Establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve the QMS  
✅ Document processes, their sequence, and interactions  

## Integration with ISO 9001:2026 Enhancements

This clause integrates:
- **Climate Change Considerations:** External issues include climate-related risks
- **Risk-Based Thinking:** Foundation for risk and opportunity identification
- **Sustainability:** Context includes environmental and social factors
- **Digital Transformation:** Technology considerations in organizational context
