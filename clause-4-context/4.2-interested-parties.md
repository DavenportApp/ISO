# 4.2 Understanding the Needs and Expectations of Interested Parties

## Requirement

The organization shall determine:
- The interested parties that are relevant to the QMS
- The requirements of these interested parties that are relevant to the QMS
- Monitor and review information about these interested parties and their requirements

## Interested Parties Identification

### Primary Interested Parties

#### Customers
**Requirements:**
- Product/service quality and reliability
- On-time delivery
- Competitive pricing
- Responsive customer service
- Compliance with specifications
- Innovation and continuous improvement
- Sustainability and ethical practices (ISO 9001:2026)

**Monitoring Methods:**
- Customer satisfaction surveys
- Feedback and complaints analysis
- Regular customer reviews
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Digital engagement metrics

#### Employees
**Requirements:**
- Safe working environment
- Fair compensation and benefits
- Professional development opportunities
- Clear roles and responsibilities
- Work-life balance
- Digital tools and modern workplace (ISO 9001:2026)
- Climate-safe workplace conditions

**Monitoring Methods:**
- Employee satisfaction surveys
- Performance reviews
- Turnover rates
- Training participation
- Safety incident reports

#### Top Management/Owners
**Requirements:**
- Financial performance
- Market competitiveness
- Regulatory compliance
- Brand reputation
- Long-term sustainability
- Risk management
- Digital transformation progress
- Climate resilience

**Monitoring Methods:**
- Financial reports
- Management reviews
- Performance dashboards
- Risk assessments
- Sustainability metrics

### Secondary Interested Parties

#### Suppliers and Partners
**Requirements:**
- Clear specifications and requirements
- Fair business practices
- Timely payments
- Long-term partnerships
- Sustainable sourcing practices
- Digital integration capabilities

**Monitoring Methods:**
- Supplier performance evaluations
- Partnership reviews
- Payment cycle tracking
- Sustainability audits

#### Regulatory and Certification Bodies
**Requirements:**
- Compliance with laws and regulations
- ISO 9001:2026 conformance
- Industry-specific standards
- Environmental regulations
- Data protection (GDPR, etc.)
- Climate-related disclosures

**Monitoring Methods:**
- Compliance audits
- Certification audits
- Regulatory updates tracking
- Legal register maintenance

#### Community and Society
**Requirements:**
- Environmental protection
- Social responsibility
- Local employment
- Community engagement
- Climate action commitments
- Ethical business practices

**Monitoring Methods:**
- Environmental impact assessments
- Community feedback
- Social responsibility reports
- Carbon footprint tracking

#### Investors and Financial Institutions
**Requirements:**
- Financial stability
- Growth potential
- Risk management
- ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) performance
- Climate risk disclosure
- Digital transformation ROI

**Monitoring Methods:**
- Financial statements
- ESG reports
- Investor communications
- Credit ratings

## Stakeholder Communication Plan

### Communication Channels

**Internal:**
- Management meetings (weekly/monthly)
- Employee newsletters (monthly)
- Town halls (quarterly)
- Digital collaboration platforms (daily)
- Intranet and knowledge base (continuous)

**External:**
- Customer portal (continuous)
- Supplier meetings (quarterly)
- Annual sustainability report
- Website and social media
- Regulatory submissions (as required)
- Climate disclosure reports (annual)

### Feedback Mechanisms

**Customer Feedback:**
- Satisfaction surveys (quarterly)
- Complaint management system (continuous)
- Customer advisory board (semi-annual)
- Digital feedback tools (real-time)

**Employee Feedback:**
- Engagement surveys (annual)
- Pulse surveys (quarterly)
- Suggestion box (continuous)
- Exit interviews (as needed)

**Stakeholder Consultation:**
- Stakeholder forums (annual)
- Focus groups (as needed)
- Public consultations (for major changes)
- Digital engagement platforms

## Requirements Analysis Process

### Step 1: Identify Stakeholders
- Brainstorm all potential interested parties
- Consider direct and indirect stakeholders
- Include ISO 9001:2026 new stakeholder categories
- Document in stakeholder register

### Step 2: Prioritize Stakeholders
**Criteria:**
- Impact on QMS success
- Influence over organization
- Dependency level
- Risk/opportunity potential
- Regulatory significance

**Priority Levels:**
- **High:** Critical to QMS success, regular engagement required
- **Medium:** Important but less frequent engagement
- **Low:** Monitor but limited active engagement

### Step 3: Determine Requirements
- Conduct stakeholder interviews
- Review contracts and agreements
- Analyze regulatory requirements
- Monitor market trends
- Assess sustainability expectations
- Evaluate digital maturity needs

### Step 4: Monitor and Review
- Regular stakeholder surveys
- Performance against requirements
- Changes in expectations
- Emerging stakeholder groups
- Climate-related expectation changes

### Step 5: Update QMS
- Integrate requirements into planning
- Adjust processes as needed
- Update quality objectives
- Modify risk assessments
- Enhance sustainability practices

```
- [Requirement 3]
Communication Frequency: [Weekly/Monthly/Quarterly]
Communication Method: [Meetings/Reports/Portal]
Contact Person: [Name and role]
Last Review Date: [Date]
Next Review Date: [Date]
```

## Review Process

**Frequency:**
- Comprehensive review: Annual
- High-priority stakeholders: Quarterly
- Medium-priority stakeholders: Semi-annual
- Continuous monitoring: Ongoing

**Review Activities:**
- Update stakeholder register
- Assess requirement changes
- Evaluate communication effectiveness
- Identify new stakeholders
- Remove obsolete stakeholders
- Update engagement strategies

## Performance Indicators

- Stakeholder satisfaction scores
- Response rate to stakeholder surveys
- Number of requirements integrated into QMS
- Timeliness of stakeholder communications
- Complaint/issue resolution time
- ESG rating scores
- Climate disclosure completeness
