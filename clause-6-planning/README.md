# Clause 6: Planning

## Overview

Planning is essential for the QMS to achieve its intended results. This clause establishes requirements for addressing risks and opportunities, setting quality objectives, and planning changes to the QMS.

## Contents

- [6.1 Actions to Address Risks and Opportunities](./6.1-risk-opportunity.md)
- [6.2 Quality Objectives and Planning to Achieve Them](./6.2-quality-objectives.md)
- [6.3 Planning of Changes](./6.3-planning-of-changes.md)

## Purpose

Clause 6 ensures that:
- Risks and opportunities are systematically identified and addressed
- Quality objectives are established, aligned with policy, and achieved
- Changes to the QMS are planned and controlled
- Continual improvement is integrated into planning

## Key Requirements

✅ Determine risks and opportunities that need to be addressed  
✅ Plan actions to address risks and opportunities  
✅ Integrate actions into QMS processes  
✅ Establish quality objectives at relevant functions and levels  
✅ Ensure objectives are measurable and monitored  
✅ Plan how to achieve quality objectives  
✅ Plan changes to the QMS in a systematic manner  

## ISO 9001:2026 Enhanced Planning Requirements

### Risk-Based Thinking Throughout
- Comprehensive risk assessment methodology
- Integration of risk management in all processes
- Proactive identification and mitigation
- Risk-aware decision making
- Opportunity exploitation strategies

### Climate Change Planning (ISO 9001:2026)
- Climate risk assessment and planning
- Climate adaptation strategies
- Carbon reduction planning
- Resilience building measures
- Long-term climate scenarios

### Digital Transformation Planning (ISO 9001:2026)
- Digital roadmap integration with QMS
- Technology adoption planning
- Cybersecurity risk planning
- Data management strategies
- Digital capability development

### Sustainability Planning (ISO 9001:2026)
- Environmental objectives setting
- Social responsibility planning
- ESG target establishment
- Circular economy initiatives
- Sustainable development goals alignment

### Quality Culture Planning
- Culture assessment and development plans
- Employee engagement initiatives
- Leadership development programs
- Communication and awareness planning
- Recognition and reward systems

## Integration Points

### With Context (Clause 4)
- Risks and opportunities derived from context analysis
- Stakeholder requirements inform objectives
- Scope defines planning boundaries

### With Leadership (Clause 5)
- Top management ensures planning is conducted
- Quality policy provides framework for objectives
- Leadership drives planning effectiveness

### With Support (Clause 7)
- Resources planned to address risks and achieve objectives
- Competence needs identified through planning
- Communication planned for objectives and changes

### With Operation (Clause 8)
- Operational planning integrates risk actions
- Objectives drive operational performance
- Changes implemented through operations

### With Performance Evaluation (Clause 9)
- Monitoring of risk treatment effectiveness
- Measurement of objective achievement
- Management review evaluates planning adequacy

### With Improvement (Clause 10)
- Planning drives continual improvement
- Improvement opportunities identified through planning
- Corrective actions planned and integrated

## Planning Documentation

### Required Documented Information

**Risk Register:**
- Identified risks and opportunities
- Assessment and prioritization
- Treatment plans and actions
- Responsibility and timelines
- Monitoring and review

**Quality Objectives:**
- Objective statements
- Alignment with policy and context
- Measurability criteria
- Target values and timelines
- Responsibility assignments

**Action Plans:**
- Actions to address risks/opportunities
- Actions to achieve objectives
- Resources required
- Implementation schedule
- Success criteria

**Change Plans:**
- Description of changes
- Purpose and intended results
- Impact assessment
- Implementation approach
- Verification methods

## Performance Indicators

- Percentage of identified risks with treatment plans
- Risk mitigation effectiveness
- Opportunity realization rate
- Quality objective achievement rate
- On-time completion of action plans
- Change implementation success rate
- Stakeholder satisfaction with planning process
- Integration of ISO 9001:2026 requirements in planning
