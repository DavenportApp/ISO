# Clause 8: Operation

## Overview

This clause covers the operational processes necessary to meet requirements and deliver products and services that satisfy customers. It addresses planning, customer requirements, design, production/service provision, release, and control of nonconforming outputs.

## Purpose

Clause 8 ensures that:
- Operations are planned and controlled to meet requirements
- Customer requirements are determined, reviewed, and met
- Design and development activities are effectively controlled
- External providers deliver conforming products and services
- Production and service provision are controlled
- Products and services are verified before release
- Nonconforming outputs are identified, controlled, and prevented from unintended use
- Digital technologies enhance operational efficiency (ISO 9001:2026)
- Environmental impacts are minimized in operations (ISO 9001:2026)

## Key Requirements

✅ Plan and control operational processes  
✅ Determine and review customer requirements  
✅ Control design and development when applicable  
✅ Manage external providers and supply chain  
✅ Control production and service provision  
✅ Verify products/services before release  
✅ Control nonconforming outputs  
✅ Implement traceability when required  
✅ Manage customer property appropriately  
✅ Preserve product/service conformity  
✅ Address post-delivery activities  
✅ Control changes to operations  
✅ Integrate digital and automation technologies (ISO 9001:2026)  
✅ Minimize environmental impact of operations (ISO 9001:2026)
