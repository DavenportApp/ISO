# 8.1 Operational Planning and Control

## Requirement

The organization shall plan, implement, and control the processes needed to meet requirements for the provision of products and services, and to implement actions determined in Clause 6, by:

- Determining requirements for products and services
- Establishing criteria for processes and acceptance of products/services
- Determining necessary resources
- Implementing process controls
- Determining, maintaining, and retaining documented information
- Controlling planned changes and mitigating unintended consequences
- Controlling outsourced processes

The output of this planning shall be suitable for the organization's operations and shall ensure that:
- Process conformity is achieved
- Product and service conformity is achieved

## Purpose

Operational planning and control ensures that:
- All necessary processes are identified and planned
- Requirements are clearly understood
- Adequate resources are provided
- Process controls are effective
- Changes are managed properly
- Outsourced processes are controlled
- Digital technologies enhance operations (ISO 9001:2026)
- Environmental impacts are minimized (ISO 9001:2026)

## Control of Changes

### Change Management Process

**Types of Changes:**
- Process changes
- Product/service design changes
- Equipment or technology changes
- Material or supplier changes
- Facility or layout changes
- Personnel or organizational changes
- Regulatory or standard changes
- Digital transformation changes (ISO 9001:2026)
- Sustainability initiatives (ISO 9001:2026)

**Change Request and Approval:**
1. **Initiate Change Request**
   - Describe proposed change
   - Justify need for change
   - Identify affected processes/products
   - Assess risks and opportunities
   - Assess environmental impact (ISO 9001:2026)

2. **Review and Assessment**
   - Technical feasibility
   - Resource requirements
   - Impact on quality
   - Impact on schedule and cost
   - Regulatory compliance
   - Customer approval if needed
   - Climate and sustainability impact (ISO 9001:2026)

3. **Approval Decision**
   - Approve, reject, or modify
   - Authorization by appropriate authority
   - Document decision and rationale
   - Digital approval workflow (ISO 9001:2026)

4. **Implementation Planning**
   - Develop implementation plan
   - Identify required actions
   - Assign responsibilities
   - Set timeline
   - Plan verification activities
   - Plan training and communication

5. **Implementation**
   - Execute change per plan
   - Verify effectiveness
   - Document implementation
   - Train personnel
   - Update documentation
   - Communicate changes

6. **Verification and Validation**
   - Verify change implemented correctly
   - Validate effectiveness
   - Monitor for unintended consequences
   - Continuous monitoring (ISO 9001:2026)

7. **Close-Out**
   - Document results
   - Lessons learned
   - Update procedures if needed
   - Communicate completion

## Control of Outsourced Processes

### Outsourced Process Identification

**Types of Outsourced Processes:**
- Manufacturing processes
- Testing and inspection
- Design and development
- Logistics and warehousing
- IT and technology services
- Customer service
- Maintenance and calibration
- Training delivery
- Cloud computing and data storage (ISO 9001:2026)

**Criticality Assessment:**
- **Critical:** Direct impact on product/service conformity
  - Higher control requirements
  - Regular audits and oversight
  - Performance monitoring
  
- **Important:** Supports critical processes
  - Moderate control requirements
  - Periodic reviews
  - Performance metrics
  
- **Supporting:** Minimal quality impact
  - Basic control requirements
  - Annual reviews
  - Service level agreements

### Outsourced Process Control

**Control Methods:**
- Clear requirements and specifications
- Supplier qualification and selection
- Service level agreements (SLAs)
- Performance monitoring and metrics
- Regular reviews and audits
- Contingency and backup plans
- Digital integration and visibility (ISO 9001:2026)

**Responsibility:**
The organization remains responsible for ensuring conformity even when processes are outsourced.

**Integration:**
- Align with overall QMS
- Include in risk assessment
- Monitor performance
- Continuous improvement
- Document control
- Communication protocols
