# 5.3 Organizational Roles, Responsibilities and Authorities

## Requirement

Top management shall ensure that responsibilities and authorities for relevant roles are assigned, communicated and understood within the organization.

Top management shall assign the responsibility and authority for:
- Ensuring the QMS conforms to ISO 9001:2026 requirements
- Ensuring processes deliver their intended outputs
- Reporting on QMS performance and improvement opportunities
- Ensuring customer focus throughout the organization
- Ensuring QMS integrity during changes

**Primary Responsibilities:**
- Overall accountability for QMS effectiveness
- Leadership and commitment to quality
- Establishing quality policy and objectives
- Ensuring customer focus
- Resource allocation for QMS
- Strategic direction and planning
- Management review chair
- Climate and sustainability leadership (ISO 9001:2026)
- Digital transformation oversight (ISO 9001:2026)

**Authorities:**
- Final decision-making authority
- Resource allocation approval
- Policy approval

**Primary Responsibilities:**
- Overall coordination of QMS
- Ensuring QMS conforms to ISO 9001:2026 requirements
- Ensuring processes deliver intended outputs

- Promoting customer focus throughout organization
- Ensuring QMS integrity during changes
- Internal audit program management
- External audit coordination
- Quality training coordination
- Document control oversight
- Continual improvement facilitation

**Authorities:**
- Stop production for quality issues
- Access to all processes and personnel
- Request resources for QMS
- Approve quality procedures
- Conduct internal audits
- Initiate corrective actions
- Report directly to top management

**Competence Requirements:**
- Deep understanding of ISO 9001:2026
- Quality management principles
- Audit skills (internal and external)
- Root cause analysis
- Process management
- Leadership and influencing skills
- Communication skills

**Reports To:** Top Management / CEO  
**Reports From:** Quality Assurance Manager, Quality Control Manager, Document Control Specialist

**ISO 9001:2026 Enhanced Responsibilities:**

- Coordinate digital quality initiatives
- Monitor sustainability integration in quality processes
- Ensure quality culture development
- Facilitate cross-functional collaboration on new requirements

## Responsibility During Changes

### Maintaining QMS Integrity

**QMR Responsibilities During Changes:**
- Assess impact of changes on QMS
- Ensure changes are planned and controlled
- Verify QMS requirements remain met
- Update QMS documentation
- Communicate changes to affected parties
- Verify effectiveness of changes

**Change Types:**
- Organizational restructuring
- New products or services
- Process changes
- Location changes
- Technology changes
- Standard updates
- Climate-related adaptations
- Digital transformations

**Change Management Process:**
1. Change request and justification
2. Impact assessment on QMS
3. Planning and approval
4. Implementation with controls
5. Verification of effectiveness
6. Documentation update
7. Communication
