# 5.2 Quality Policy

## Requirement

Top management shall establish, implement and maintain a quality policy that:
- Is appropriate to the purpose and context of the organization
- Provides a framework for setting quality objectives
- Includes a commitment to satisfy applicable requirements
- Includes a commitment to continual improvement of the QMS

The quality policy shall:
- Be available as documented information
- Be communicated within the organization
- Be available to relevant interested parties, as appropriate

**Our Commitment to Quality**

At [Organization Name], we are committed to delivering products and services that consistently meet and exceed customer expectations while maintaining the highest standards of quality management.

**Quality Principles:**

1. **Customer Focus**
   - We prioritize understanding and meeting customer needs
   - We strive for excellence in customer satisfaction
   - We build lasting relationships based on trust and quality

2. **Leadership and Commitment**
   - Top management is personally accountable for quality
   - We promote a culture of quality throughout the organization
   - We lead by example in demonstrating quality behaviors

3. **Engagement of People**
   - We empower and engage all employees in quality
   - We provide competence development and training
   - We recognize and reward quality achievements

4. **Process Approach**
   - We manage our activities as interconnected processes
   - We use data and analysis to drive decisions
   - We apply risk-based thinking in all our processes

5. **Continual Improvement**
   - We continuously improve our products, services, and processes
   - We learn from both successes and failures
   - We innovate to meet evolving customer and market needs

6. **Evidence-Based Decision Making**
   - We make decisions based on data analysis
   - We measure and monitor our performance
   - We use information effectively to drive improvements

7. **Relationship Management**
   - We build mutually beneficial relationships with suppliers
   - We collaborate with stakeholders for shared success
   - We maintain transparent communication with all parties

**ISO 9001:2026 Enhanced Commitments:**

8. **Climate Responsibility**
   - We acknowledge and address climate-related risks and opportunities
   - We integrate climate considerations into our quality management
   - We strive to minimize our environmental impact
   - We adapt our operations for climate resilience

9. **Digital Excellence**
   - We leverage digital technologies to enhance quality
   - We ensure data integrity and cybersecurity in our operations
   - We use digital tools for continuous monitoring and improvement
   - We foster digital competence across our organization

10. **Sustainability Integration**
    - We balance economic, environmental, and social objectives
    - We promote sustainable practices throughout our value chain
    - We contribute to the UN Sustainable Development Goals
    - We operate ethically and responsibly in all our activities

**Our Commitments:**

✅ **Compliance:** We comply with all applicable statutory, regulatory, and customer requirements  
✅ **Continual Improvement:** We continuously improve the effectiveness of our Quality Management System  
✅ **Stakeholder Satisfaction:** We strive to exceed the expectations of all our stakeholders  
✅ **Quality Objectives:** We establish, monitor, and achieve measurable quality objectives  
✅ **Resource Provision:** We provide adequate resources to maintain and improve our QMS  
✅ **Climate Action:** We take meaningful action to address climate change impacts  
✅ **Digital Transformation:** We embrace digital technologies for quality excellence  
✅ **Sustainable Operations:** We operate in an environmentally and socially responsible manner  

This policy provides the framework for establishing and reviewing our quality objectives and is communicated to all personnel and relevant interested parties.

## Policy Development Process

### Step 1: Context Analysis
- Review organizational context (Clause 4.1)
- Consider interested party requirements (Clause 4.2)
- Assess strategic direction
- Evaluate current performance
- Consider ISO 9001:2026 requirements

### Step 2: Policy Drafting
- Align with organizational purpose
- Include all required commitments
- Use clear, understandable language
- Ensure measurability through objectives
- Incorporate ISO 9001:2026 enhancements
- Review with stakeholders

### Step 3: Approval
- Present to top management
- Review and discuss
- Obtain formal approval
- Document approval with signature and date

### Step 4: Communication
- Internal communication to all personnel
- Include in employee onboarding
- Display prominently in facilities
- Publish on intranet and website
- Include in quality manual
- Communicate to suppliers and partners

### Step 5: Implementation
- Integrate into daily operations
- Link to quality objectives
- Reference in procedures and processes
- Use in training and awareness programs
- Embed in performance management

## Policy Review and Update

### Review Triggers

**Scheduled Reviews:**
- Annual management review
- Strategic planning cycles
- Certification audits

**Event-Triggered Reviews:**
- Significant organizational changes
- Changes in context or strategy
- New regulatory requirements
- Major customer requirement changes
- Changes in ISO 9001 standard
- Climate-related developments
- Digital transformation milestones
- Sustainability goal updates

### Review Process

1. **Prepare Review Data:**
   - QMS performance data
   - Customer satisfaction results
   - Context and stakeholder analysis
   - Compliance status
   - Quality objective achievement
   - Climate performance
   - Digital transformation progress
   - Sustainability metrics

2. **Conduct Review:**
   - Management review meeting
   - Assess continued appropriateness
   - Evaluate effectiveness
   - Identify improvement opportunities
   - Consider ISO 9001:2026 alignment

3. **Update if Needed:**
   - Draft revisions
   - Stakeholder consultation
   - Approval process
   - Version control
   - Communication of changes

4. **Document Review:**
   - Review meeting minutes
   - Decision rationale
   - Changes made (if any)
   - Next review date

## Alignment with Quality Objectives

The quality policy provides the framework for setting quality objectives by:

1. **Defining Direction:** Policy principles guide objective areas
2. **Setting Standards:** Policy commitments establish minimum standards
3. **Providing Focus:** Policy priorities inform objective priorities
4. **Enabling Measurement:** Policy statements enable objective metrics
5. **Ensuring Consistency:** Policy ensures objectives align with strategy

Example Linkage:
- **Policy:** "We strive for excellence in customer satisfaction"
- **Objective:** "Achieve customer satisfaction score of ≥90% by Q4 2026"

## Performance Indicators

- Policy awareness level (% employees who can explain policy)
- Policy alignment with context (assessment score)
- Quality objective derivation from policy (% coverage)
- Stakeholder satisfaction with policy
- Policy review completion (on schedule)
- Policy effectiveness in driving behavior change
