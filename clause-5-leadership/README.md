# Clause 5: Leadership

## Overview

Leadership and commitment from top management is fundamental to the success of the Quality Management System. This clause establishes the requirements for leadership involvement, quality policy, and organizational roles.

## Contents

- [5.1 Leadership and Commitment](./5.1-leadership-commitment.md)
- [5.2 Quality Policy](./5.2-quality-policy.md)
- [5.3 Organizational Roles, Responsibilities and Authorities](./5.3-roles-responsibilities.md)

## Purpose

Clause 5 ensures that:
- Top management demonstrates leadership and commitment to the QMS
- A quality policy is established and communicated
- Roles, responsibilities, and authorities are defined and understood
- Quality culture is promoted throughout the organization (ISO 9001:2026)

## Key Requirements

✅ Top management accountability for QMS effectiveness  
✅ Customer focus integrated into business strategy  
✅ Quality policy established, documented, and communicated  
✅ Quality policy aligned with organizational context and strategy  
✅ Roles and responsibilities assigned and communicated  
✅ Quality culture promoted and maintained (ISO 9001:2026)  
✅ Leadership visibility and active engagement (ISO 9001:2026)  

## ISO 9001:2026 Enhanced Leadership Requirements

### Quality Culture Development
- Active promotion of quality values
- Leadership visibility and role modeling
- Employee engagement and empowerment
- Recognition and reward systems
- Continuous learning environment

### Strategic Integration
- Quality integrated into business strategy
- Climate considerations in strategic planning
- Digital transformation leadership
- Sustainability commitments from top management
- ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) leadership

### Enhanced Accountability
- Personal accountability for QMS results
- Visible commitment to improvement
- Resource allocation authority
- Stakeholder engagement
- Culture of transparency and integrity

## Integration Points

- **Clause 4:** Leadership ensures context and stakeholder requirements are addressed
- **Clause 6:** Leadership drives planning and sets quality objectives
- **Clause 7:** Leadership ensures adequate resources and support
- **Clause 9:** Leadership conducts management reviews
- **Clause 10:** Leadership champions continual improvement

## Leadership Behaviors (ISO 9001:2026)

### Expected Leadership Attributes

**1. Visible Commitment**
- Regular quality performance reviews
- Participation in quality initiatives
- Communication of quality importance
- Resource allocation decisions
- Recognition of quality achievements

**2. Strategic Thinking**
- Long-term quality vision
- Integration with business goals
- Risk-based decision making
- Innovation encouragement
- Sustainability mindset

**3. People Development**
- Investment in competence
- Empowerment and delegation
- Coaching and mentoring
- Career development support
- Diversity and inclusion

**4. Customer Focus**
- Understanding customer needs
- Direct customer engagement
- Customer satisfaction monitoring
- Voice of customer in decisions
- Customer-centric culture

**5. Improvement Champion**
- Challenging status quo
- Supporting innovation
- Learning from failures
- Celebrating successes
- Driving continuous improvement
