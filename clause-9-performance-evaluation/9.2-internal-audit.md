# 9.2 Internal Audit

## 9.2.1 General

### Requirement

The organization shall conduct internal audits at planned intervals to provide information on whether the QMS:
- Conforms to:
  - The organization's own requirements for its QMS
  - The requirements of ISO 9001:2026
- Is effectively implemented and maintained

## 9.2.2 Internal Audit Programme

### Requirement

The organization shall:
- Plan, establish, implement, and maintain an audit programme including frequency, methods, responsibilities, planning requirements, and reporting
- Define audit criteria and scope for each audit
- Select auditors and conduct audits to ensure objectivity and impartiality
- Ensure audit results are reported to relevant management
- Take appropriate correction and corrective actions without undue delay
- Retain documented information as evidence

## Purpose of Internal Audits

Internal audits provide:
- Verification of QMS conformity and effectiveness
- Identification of improvement opportunities
- Assurance to management and stakeholders
- Preparation for external audits
- Promotion of continual improvement culture
- Risk identification and mitigation
- Digital system verification (ISO 9001:2026)
- Sustainability compliance assessment (ISO 9001:2026)

## Internal Audit Programme

### Annual Audit Schedule

**Risk-Based Audit Frequency:**
- **High Risk Areas:** Quarterly
  - Customer-facing processes
  - Critical production processes
  - New processes or changes
  - Areas with past issues
  - Regulatory compliance areas
  
- **Medium Risk Areas:** Semi-annually
  - Support processes
  - Stable processes
  - Moderate complexity
  
- **Low Risk Areas:** Annually
  - Mature, stable processes
  - Administrative processes
  - Low impact areas

**Audit Schedule Factors:**
- Process importance and risks
- Previous audit results
- Changes and improvement actions
- External audit findings
- Customer feedback
- Regulatory requirements
- Organizational changes
- Digital transformation progress (ISO 9001:2026)

### Audit Planning

**Annual Plan Development:**
1. **Identify Scope:** All QMS processes and requirements
2. **Assess Risks:** Determine criticality and frequency
3. **Schedule Audits:** Distribute throughout year
4. **Assign Resources:** Auditors and time
5. **Communicate Plan:** To management and auditees
6. **Review and Approve:** Management approval
7. **Maintain Flexibility:** Allow for unplanned audits

**Individual Audit Planning:**
1. **Determine Objectives:** What to verify
2. **Define Scope:** Processes, sites, standards
3. **Select Audit Team:** Competent and independent auditors
4. **Review Background:** Previous audits, performance data
5. **Prepare Checklist:** Based on requirements and risks
6. **Schedule Audit:** Coordinate with auditee
7. **Notify Auditee:** Advance notice of audit
8. **Prepare Documents:** Audit plan, checklist, forms

## Auditor Competence and Independence

### Auditor Requirements

**Knowledge:**
- ISO 9001:2026 standard requirements
- QMS principles and concepts
- Audit principles and processes
- Organization's processes and products
- Applicable regulations and standards
- Digital systems and technologies (ISO 9001:2026)
- Sustainability and climate considerations (ISO 9001:2026)

**Skills:**
- Audit planning and preparation
- Interviewing and communication
- Observation and evidence gathering
- Analysis and evaluation
- Report writing
- Diplomacy and objectivity
- Digital audit tools proficiency (ISO 9001:2026)

**Personal Attributes:**
- Ethical and professional
- Open-minded and impartial
- Diplomatic and tactful
- Observant and perceptive
- Versatile and resilient
- Decisive and confident
- Self-reliant and collaborative

### Auditor Training and Development

**Training Path:**
1. **Foundation:** ISO 9001 requirements training
2. **Auditing Principles:** Internal auditor course
3. **Practical Experience:** Shadow experienced auditors
4. **Lead Audits:** Supervised audits
5. **Independent:** Competence demonstrated
6. **Continuous Development:** Advanced training, updates
7. **Digital Auditing:** Remote audit techniques (ISO 9001:2026)
8. **Sustainability Auditing:** ESG and climate auditing (ISO 9001:2026)

**Ongoing Development:**
- ISO standard updates training
- Advanced audit techniques
- Industry-specific knowledge
- Process-specific training
- Digital transformation topics (ISO 9001:2026)
- Calibration and consistency sessions
- Audit team meetings and feedback

### Independence and Objectivity

**Auditor Independence Rules:**
- Auditors shall not audit their own work
- Minimum 1 year separation from audited area
- No direct reporting relationship
- No recent responsibility for audited area
- No conflicts of interest
- External auditors for sensitive areas

**Maintaining Objectivity:**
- Focus on objective evidence
- Avoid bias and assumptions
- Challenge respectfully
- Document facts, not opinions
- Consistent criteria application
- Separate facts from conclusions

## Audit Process

### Step 1: Opening Meeting

**Objectives:**
- Introduce audit team
- Confirm audit scope and objectives
- Explain audit process and schedule
- Address questions and concerns
- Establish communication channels
- Set expectations for evidence

**Attendees:**
- Audit team
- Auditee management
- Process owners
- Management representative

### Step 2: Audit Execution

**Evidence Gathering Methods:**
- **Interviews:** Ask open-ended questions, listen actively
- **Observation:** Watch work being performed
- **Document Review:** Check procedures, records, data
- **Physical Inspection:** Examine products, facilities, equipment
- **System Review:** Check digital systems and data (ISO 9001:2026)
- **Data Analysis:** Review performance metrics and trends

**Audit Trail:**
- Sample transactions from start to finish
- Verify requirements flow through process
- Check input-process-output linkage
- Trace documents and records
- Verify digital traceability (ISO 9001:2026)

**Note-Taking:**
- Record objective evidence
- Note positive observations
- Document non-conformities clearly
- Include who, what, where, when
- Reference specific requirements
- Digital note-taking tools (ISO 9001:2026)

### Step 3: Findings Classification

**Conformity:** Meeting requirements
- Note positive practices
- Recognize good performance
- Identify best practices to share

**Observation/Opportunity:** Area for improvement
- Not a non-conformity yet
- Risk of future non-conformity
- Improvement potential
- Innovation opportunities (ISO 9001:2026)

**Minor Non-Conformity:**
- Single, isolated failure to meet requirement
- Does not significantly impact QMS effectiveness
- Not systemic or widespread
- Recent occurrence

**Major Non-Conformity:**
- Complete absence of requirement
- Systematic failure (multiple instances)
- Significant impact on QMS or product/service
- Breakdown of process control
- Serious risk to quality, safety, or compliance

### Step 4: Closing Meeting

**Objectives:**
- Present audit findings
- Explain evidence and conclusions
- Discuss non-conformities
- Agree on findings (or note disagreements)
- Clarify corrective action expectations
- Discuss timeline for responses
- Thank auditee for cooperation

**Attendees:**
- Audit team
- Auditee management
- Process owners
- Management representative
- Top management (for major findings)

### Step 5: Audit Reporting

**Audit Report Content:**
- Audit details (date, scope, team, auditee)
- Audit objectives and criteria
- Executive summary
- Positive findings and conformities
- Observations and opportunities
- Non-conformities (with evidence and requirement)
- Conclusion on QMS effectiveness
- Distribution list
- Digital report format (ISO 9001:2026)

**Report Distribution:**
- Auditee
- Auditee's management
- Management representative
- Top management (summary)
- Quality file and records

**Report Timeline:**
- Draft within 1 week of audit
- Final report within 2 weeks
- Documented information retained
- Real-time reporting option (ISO 9001:2026)

### Step 6: Corrective Action and Follow-Up

**Auditee Responsibilities:**
- Review findings
- Determine root causes
- Develop corrective actions
- Implement corrections and corrective actions
- Provide evidence of completion
- Target: Within 30 days for minor, 60 days for major

**Auditor Follow-Up:**
- Review corrective action plans
- Verify implementation
- Confirm effectiveness
- Close findings when verified
- Follow-up audit if needed
- Digital tracking and verification (ISO 9001:2026)

## Best Practices

### Internal Audit Excellence

**1. Plan Thoroughly:**
- Risk-based approach
- Adequate time allocation
- Competent auditors
- Good communication with auditees
- Review background information

**2. Focus on Effectiveness:**
- Not just compliance checking
- Evaluate if processes achieve intended results
- Look for improvement opportunities
- Verify customer satisfaction impact
- Assess sustainability performance (ISO 9001:2026)

**3. Be Professional:**
- Respectful and courteous
- Objective and fair
- Evidence-based conclusions
- Collaborative approach
- Constructive feedback

**4. Value-Add Auditing:**
- Identify best practices
- Share across organization
- Facilitate improvement
- Build relationships
- Support auditees' success

**5. Leverage Technology:**
- Digital audit tools
- Remote audit capabilities
- Automated data analysis
- Real-time reporting
- Continuous monitoring

**6. Continuous Improvement:**
- Audit the audit programme
- Auditor feedback and development
- Benchmark best practices
- Innovate audit approaches
- Learn from external audits

## Common Challenges and Solutions

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| Auditor competence gaps | Training programme; external training; mentoring; certifications |
| Lack of independence | Rotation; external auditors; clear rules; oversight |
| Audit fatigue | Efficient audits; value demonstration; positive approach; recognition |
| Poor auditee preparation | Clear expectations; checklists provided; pre-audit meetings |
| Defensive auditees | Collaborative approach; focus on improvement; confidentiality |
| Insufficient follow-up | Tracking system; escalation process; management accountability |
| Time and resource constraints | Risk-based prioritization; efficient methods; digital tools |
| Resistance to remote audits | Training; demonstrate effectiveness; hybrid approach; pilot projects |
