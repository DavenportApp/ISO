# 7.3 Awareness

## Requirement

The organization shall ensure that persons doing work under the organization's control are aware of:
- The quality policy
- Relevant quality objectives
- Their contribution to the effectiveness of the QMS, including the benefits of improved performance
- The implications of not conforming with the QMS requirements

## Purpose of Awareness

Awareness goes beyond just knowing—it's about understanding the **"why"** behind quality requirements and how individual actions contribute to organizational success and customer satisfaction.

### Awareness vs. Knowledge vs. Competence

| Aspect | Knowledge | Awareness | Competence |
|--------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| Focus | What to do | Why it matters | Ability to do it well |
| Level | Information | Understanding | Application |
| Outcome | Can explain | Motivated to act | Performs effectively |
| Measurement | Tests, quizzes | Surveys, observation | Performance results |

## Elements of Quality Awareness

### 1. Quality Policy Awareness

**What People Should Know:**
- Content of the quality policy
- How it applies to their work
- Connection to organizational values and strategy
- Their role in supporting the policy
- How quality policy guides decisions

**Awareness Activities:**
- Quality policy communication sessions
- Display in prominent locations
- Include in employee handbook
- Reference in meetings and communications
- Integrate into onboarding
- Annual policy refreshers
- Digital accessibility (intranet, app) (ISO 9001:2026)

**Verification Methods:**
- Random awareness surveys
- Policy understanding questions
- Observation of decision-making
- Employee interviews
- Integration in performance reviews

### 2. Quality Objectives Awareness

**What People Should Know:**
- Organizational quality objectives
- Departmental and team objectives
- How objectives are measured
- Current performance status
- How their work contributes to objectives
- Personal objectives aligned with QMS (ISO 9001:2026)

**Awareness Activities:**
- Objectives cascade and communication
- Visual management boards
- Performance dashboards
- Team meetings and huddles
- Individual objective setting
- Progress celebrations
- Real-time digital dashboards (ISO 9001:2026)

**Verification Methods:**
- Can state relevant objectives
- Understand how their work contributes
- Aware of current performance
- Engaged in improvement activities
- Takes ownership of results

### 3. Contribution to QMS Effectiveness

**What People Should Know:**
- How their role impacts quality
- Connection between their work and customer satisfaction
- Impact of their work on other processes
- Importance of following procedures
- Value of their suggestions and improvements
- How quality contributes to organizational success

**Awareness Activities:**
- Process mapping showing interdependencies
- Customer feedback sharing
- Success stories and case studies
- Quality impact communications
- Employee recognition programs
- Cross-functional exposure
- Impact visualization tools (ISO 9001:2026)

**Verification Methods:**
- Employees can describe their quality impact
- Proactive quality behaviors observed
- Voluntary participation in improvement
- Quality-focused suggestions
- Pride in quality work

### 4. Benefits of Improved Performance

**What People Should Know:**
- Customer satisfaction improvements
- Operational efficiency gains
- Cost reductions and waste elimination
- Enhanced reputation and competitiveness
- Job security and career opportunities
- Personal growth and skill development
- Positive environmental impact (ISO 9001:2026)
- Climate resilience benefits (ISO 9001:2026)

**Awareness Activities:**
- Share improvement results
- Connect improvements to benefits
- Celebrate successes
- Communicate customer feedback
- Highlight career development
- Recognition and rewards
- Sustainability wins communication (ISO 9001:2026)

**Verification Methods:**
- Employees advocate for improvements
- Positive attitude toward change
- Active participation in improvement
- Understanding of personal benefits
- Pride in organizational achievements

### 5. Implications of Non-Conformity

**What People Should Know:**
- Definition of conformity and non-conformity
- Examples relevant to their work
- Consequences of not following requirements:
  - Customer dissatisfaction
  - Product recalls or service failures
  - Increased costs and rework
  - Regulatory penalties
  - Safety risks
  - Reputation damage
  - Job implications
  - Environmental harm (ISO 9001:2026)
  - Climate-related risks (ISO 9001:2026)
- Responsibility to report non-conformities
- Non-punitive reporting culture

**Awareness Activities:**
- Real examples and case studies
- Near-miss and incident reviews
- Root cause analysis participation
- Transparent communication of issues
- Lessons learned sessions
- Non-conformity cost education
- Just culture training (ISO 9001:2026)

**Verification Methods:**
- Timely reporting of issues
- Understanding of consequences
- Compliance with procedures
- Proactive problem identification
- Learning from mistakes

## Awareness Program Development

### Step 1: Identify Awareness Needs

**Consider:**
- QMS requirements
- Organizational changes
- New processes or technologies
- Incident and non-conformity trends
- Audit findings
- Employee feedback
- Digital transformation initiatives (ISO 9001:2026)
- Climate and sustainability goals (ISO 9001:2026)
- Stakeholder expectations (ISO 9001:2026)

**Assessment Methods:**
- Awareness surveys
- Knowledge assessments
- Observation and interviews
- Audit findings analysis
- Performance data review
- Suggestion box themes
- Exit interviews

### Step 2: Design Awareness Activities

**Communication Channels:**

**Traditional:**
- Meetings (all-hands, team, one-on-one)
- Training sessions and workshops
- Posters and bulletin boards
- Employee handbook
- Newsletters and emails
- Quality noticeboards
- Presentations and briefings

**Digital (ISO 9001:2026):**
- Intranet and employee portals
- Email and instant messaging
- Mobile apps and push notifications
- Video messages and webinars
- E-learning modules and microlearning
- Social collaboration platforms
- Digital signage and dashboards
- Virtual reality experiences
- Gamification and interactive learning
- Podcasts and video series

**Engagement Methods:**
- Interactive workshops
- Gamification and competitions
- Quality quiz campaigns
- Story-telling and case studies
- Role-playing and simulations
- Cross-functional exchanges
- Quality days and events
- Recognition ceremonies
- Feedback and dialogue sessions
- Lunch-and-learn sessions

### Step 3: Deliver Awareness Communications

**Timing:**
- **Onboarding:** Quality orientation for new hires
- **Regular:** Ongoing reinforcement and updates
- **Just-in-Time:** Before changes or new processes
- **Reactive:** After incidents or findings
- **Proactive:** Anticipate future needs

**Frequency:**
- Daily: Visual management, digital feeds
- Weekly: Team huddles, newsletters
- Monthly: Department meetings, updates
- Quarterly: All-hands meetings, reviews
- Annual: Policy refresh, strategic updates
- Ad-hoc: As needed for changes or issues

**Delivery Best Practices:**
- **Clear and Concise:** Simple language, avoid jargon
- **Relevant:** Connect to employee's role and context
- **Engaging:** Interactive, visual, story-driven
- **Multi-Channel:** Use various methods to reinforce
- **Two-Way:** Enable questions and feedback
- **Consistent:** Regular rhythm and messaging
- **Authentic:** Genuine, transparent, from leadership
- **Accessible:** Available to all employees, inclusive

### Step 4: Verify Awareness

**Verification Methods:**

**Direct Assessment:**
- Awareness surveys and questionnaires
- Quiz and knowledge checks
- Interviews and focus groups
- Spot checks and random sampling
- Verbal confirmation during audits

**Indirect Assessment:**
- Observation of behaviors
- Quality of work output
- Participation in improvement activities
- Proactive reporting of issues
- Questions and suggestions quality
- Compliance with procedures
- Customer feedback themes
- Performance metrics

**Survey Example Questions:**
1. What is our quality policy? (Can you summarize it?)
2. What are the key quality objectives for your area?
3. How does your work contribute to quality and customer satisfaction?
4. What benefits have you seen from recent improvements?
5. What could happen if you don't follow quality procedures?
6. How do you report quality issues?
7. How does our organization address climate change? (ISO 9001:2026)
8. What digital tools support your quality work? (ISO 9001:2026)

### Step 5: Improve Awareness Program

**Continuous Improvement:**
- Analyze verification results
- Identify awareness gaps
- Gather feedback on communications
- Benchmark best practices
- Test new communication methods
- Update content and approaches
- Measure engagement and retention
- Celebrate awareness improvements

## Best Practices for Awareness

### 1. Leadership Visibility
- Leaders communicate quality importance
- Visible commitment and role modeling
- Regular leadership communications
- Town halls and skip-level meetings
- Leader storytelling and sharing

### 2. Make it Personal
- Connect quality to individual roles
- Show personal impact and benefits
- Recognize individual contributions
- Share relatable stories and examples
- Use peer-to-peer communication

### 3. Keep it Simple
- Clear, jargon-free language
- Visual communication (infographics, videos)
- Short, focused messages
- One key message at a time
- Repetition with variation

### 4. Make it Interactive
- Two-way dialogue, not just broadcast
- Q&A opportunities
- Feedback mechanisms
- Participation activities
- Gamification and challenges

### 5. Use Multiple Channels
- Don't rely on single channel
- Mix traditional and digital
- Accommodate different learning styles
- Ensure accessibility for all
- Redundancy for critical messages

### 6. Measure and Improve
- Regular awareness assessments
- Track communication effectiveness
- Gather feedback continuously
- Experiment with new approaches
- Share what works across organization

### 7. Leverage Technology (ISO 9001:2026)
- Mobile-first communications
- Push notifications for important updates
- Interactive e-learning
- Virtual reality for immersive learning
- AI chatbots for Q&A
- Social collaboration platforms
- Real-time performance dashboards

### 8. Integrate Sustainability (ISO 9001:2026)
- Link quality to environmental impact
- Communicate climate actions
- Show sustainability achievements
- Engage in environmental initiatives
- Share green innovation stories

## Common Challenges and Solutions

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| Information overload | Prioritize key messages; use microlearning; space out communications |
| Low engagement | Make it relevant and personal; use stories; interactive methods |
| Diverse workforce | Multi-channel approach; translate languages; accommodate disabilities |
| Remote workers | Digital tools; virtual events; mobile accessibility; regular check-ins |
| Message retention | Repetition; visual aids; application activities; spaced reinforcement |
| Competing priorities | Integrate into regular activities; leadership emphasis; show business value |
| Awareness fatigue | Vary approaches; keep fresh; celebrate milestones; make it fun |
| Cultural differences | Culturally sensitive communications; local customization; inclusive approach |
