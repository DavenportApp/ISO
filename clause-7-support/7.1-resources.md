# 7.1 Resources

## Requirement

The organization shall determine and provide the resources needed for the establishment, implementation, maintenance, and continual improvement of the quality management system.

## 7.1.1 General

### Resource Planning Process

**Step 1: Identify Requirements**
- QMS process requirements
- Quality objectives and targets
- Customer and regulatory requirements
- Organizational growth and change needs
- Digital transformation needs (ISO 9001:2026)
- Climate adaptation requirements (ISO 9001:2026)

**Step 2: Assess Current Resources**
- People (quantity and competence)
- Infrastructure and facilities
- Technology and equipment
- Financial resources
- Information and knowledge
- Supplier and partner capabilities

**Step 3: Gap Analysis**
- Compare current vs. required resources
- Identify shortfalls and excesses
- Evaluate resource efficiency
- Assess digital capability gaps
- Evaluate environmental resource needs

**Step 4: Resource Allocation**
- Prioritize based on risk and impact
- Develop resource acquisition plan
- Budget allocation
- Timeline for resource deployment
- Continuous resource optimization

### Resource Categories

**Critical Resources:**
- Essential for core processes
- High impact on quality and customer satisfaction
- Difficult to replace or substitute
- Requires priority allocation and protection

**Supporting Resources:**
- Enables effective process operation
- Moderate impact on quality
- Some flexibility in allocation
- Can be optimized over time

**Enhancement Resources:**
- Improves efficiency and capability
- Long-term competitive advantage
- Innovation and improvement
- Strategic investment

## 7.1.5 Monitoring and Measuring Resources

### General Requirements

The organization must determine and provide resources needed to ensure valid and reliable monitoring and measurement results.

### Measurement Equipment

**Types of Equipment:**
- Measuring instruments (micrometers, calipers, gauges)
- Test equipment (multimeters, oscilloscopes, analyzers)
- Inspection devices (microscopes, cameras, scanners)
- Environmental monitors (thermometers, humidity meters)
- Software and digital measurement tools
- Customer satisfaction measurement tools

**Equipment Requirements:**
- Suitable for type of monitoring/measurement
- Capable of required accuracy and precision
- Maintained and calibrated
- Protected from damage and deterioration
- Traceable to international or national standards

### Calibration Management

**Calibration Program:**
- Identification of equipment requiring calibration
- Calibration frequency determination
- Calibration procedures and standards
- Calibration laboratory selection (internal or external)
- Calibration records and certificates
- Status identification (labels, stickers, digital records)

**Calibration Process:**
1. **Schedule:** Track calibration due dates
2. **Prepare:** Clean and prepare equipment
3. **Calibrate:** Perform calibration against standards
4. **Verify:** Check results meet requirements
5. **Document:** Record results and any adjustments
6. **Status:** Label equipment with calibration status
7. **Return:** Return to service or take corrective action

**Out-of-Calibration:**
- Remove from service immediately
- Identify and assess affected measurements
- Evaluate impact on product/service quality
- Notify affected customers if necessary
- Investigate root cause
- Take corrective action
- Re-calibrate or replace equipment

### Digital Measurement Resources (ISO 9001:2026)

**Software Validation:**
- Validation of software used for measurement
- Version control and configuration management
- Regular verification of calculations

- Regular updates and patches

**Automated Measurement Systems:**

## 7.1.6 Organizational Knowledge

### Requirement

The organization shall determine the knowledge necessary for the operation of its processes and to achieve conformity of products and services.

### Knowledge Identification

**Internal Knowledge Sources:**
- Employee expertise and experience
- Lessons learned from projects
- Process improvement insights
- Problem-solving outcomes
- Innovation and research
- Customer feedback and complaints
- Audit findings and corrective actions
- Best practices and standard work

**External Knowledge Sources:**
- Industry standards and guidelines
- Academic and technical publications
- Conferences and professional networks
- Benchmarking studies
- Customer and supplier partnerships
- Consultant expertise
- Regulatory guidance
- Technology trends and innovations

### Knowledge Management Process

**1. Knowledge Capture:**
- Document critical processes and procedures
- Record expert knowledge before retirement
- Capture lessons learned from projects
- Document problem-solving approaches
- Record customer insights and feedback
- Store research and development findings
- Archive quality records and data

**2. Knowledge Organization:**
- Categorize knowledge by topic and process
- Create searchable databases and repositories
- Develop taxonomies and metadata
- Link related knowledge items
- Organize by accessibility needs
- Prioritize critical knowledge

**3. Knowledge Sharing:**
- Training and development programs
- Mentoring and coaching
- Communities of practice
- Knowledge sharing sessions
- Documentation and procedures
- Collaboration platforms
- Cross-functional teams

**4. Knowledge Application:**
- Make knowledge accessible when needed
- Integrate into work processes
- Support decision-making
- Enable problem-solving
- Drive innovation
- Reduce errors and rework

**5. Knowledge Maintenance:**
- Regular review and updates
- Remove obsolete information
- Verify accuracy and relevance
- Update for changes
- Refresh based on new learning
- Archive historical knowledge
