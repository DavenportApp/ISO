# Clause 7: Support

## Overview

This clause establishes the requirements for resources, competence, awareness, communication, and documented information necessary to support the effective implementation and operation of the Quality Management System.

## Purpose

Clause 7 ensures that:
- Adequate resources are available for QMS operation and improvement
- Personnel are competent to perform work affecting quality
- People are aware of quality policy, objectives, and their contributions
- Appropriate communication processes are established
- Documented information is created, maintained, and controlled
- Digital and technological resources support quality objectives (ISO 9001:2026)

## Key Requirements

✅ Determine and provide necessary resources  
✅ Ensure competent personnel through training and development  
✅ Create awareness of quality policy and individual contributions  
✅ Establish effective internal and external communication  
✅ Control documented information throughout its lifecycle  
✅ Leverage digital tools for knowledge management (ISO 9001:2026)  
✅ Provide resources for climate adaptation and sustainability (ISO 9001:2026)  
✅ Ensure cybersecurity and data protection capabilities (ISO 9001:2026)
