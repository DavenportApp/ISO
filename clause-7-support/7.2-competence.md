# 7.2 Competence

## Requirement

The organization shall:
- Determine necessary competence of persons doing work under its control that affects QMS performance and effectiveness
- Ensure these persons are competent based on appropriate education, training, or experience
- Where applicable, take actions to acquire necessary competence and evaluate effectiveness
- Retain appropriate documented information as evidence of competence

## Understanding Competence

### Competence Definition

**Competence =** Knowledge + Skills + Experience + Behavior + Attitude

- **Knowledge:** Understanding of principles, facts, and information
- **Skills:** Ability to apply knowledge to perform tasks
- **Experience:** Practical application and refinement over time
- **Behavior:** Actions and conduct in the workplace
- **Attitude:** Mindset and approach to work and quality

### Competence vs. Qualification

| Aspect | Qualification | Competence |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| Definition | Formal credentials or certifications | Demonstrated ability to perform |
| Focus | What you know | What you can do |
| Evidence | Certificates, degrees, licenses | Performance, results, assessments |
| Time-based | Point in time achievement | Ongoing demonstration |
| Scope | General knowledge in field | Specific job requirements |

## Competence Management Process

### Step 1: Determine Required Competence

**Identify Roles Affecting Quality:**
- Production and operations personnel
- Quality assurance and control staff
- Design and development engineers
- Management and supervisors
- Customer service representatives
- Purchasing and supplier management
- Maintenance and calibration technicians
- Internal auditors
- Any role impacting QMS effectiveness

**Define Competence Requirements:**
For each role, specify:
- Educational requirements (degrees, certifications)
- Technical skills and knowledge
- Experience requirements (years, types)
- Behavioral competencies
- Digital and technical proficiencies (ISO 9001:2026)
- Sustainability and climate awareness (ISO 9001:2026)
- Regulatory and compliance knowledge

**Create Competency Matrix:**
```
Role: Quality Inspector

Required Competencies:
1. Education: High school diploma minimum, technical certification preferred
2. Technical Skills:
   - Blueprint reading
   - Use of measurement instruments (calipers, micrometers)
   - Understanding of GD&T (Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing)
   - Statistical sampling methods
3. Experience: Minimum 2 years in quality inspection or related field
4. Knowledge:
   - ISO 9001 requirements
   - Product specifications and standards
   - Inspection procedures and methods
   - Nonconformance reporting
5. Behavioral:
   - Attention to detail
   - Integrity and ethics
   - Communication skills
   - Problem-solving ability
6. Digital Skills (ISO 9001:2026):
   - Quality management software
   - Statistical analysis tools
   - Digital measurement systems
   - Data recording and reporting
```

### Step 2: Assess Current Competence

**Assessment Methods:**

**1. Documentation Review:**
- Education certificates and degrees
- Professional certifications
- Training records
- Work history and resume
- Performance appraisals
- Previous competency assessments

**2. Practical Evaluation:**
- Skills demonstrations
- Work sampling and observation
- Simulations and scenarios
- Pilot projects
- Probationary period assessment
- Peer review

**3. Testing and Examination:**
- Written knowledge tests
- Practical skills tests
- Oral examinations
- Computer-based assessments
- Certification exams
- Digital proficiency tests

**4. Self-Assessment:**
- Competency self-rating
- Skills inventory
- Development needs identification
- Career aspirations
- Learning preferences

**5. Multi-Rater Feedback (360°):**
- Supervisor assessment
- Peer feedback
- Subordinate input (for managers)
- Customer feedback
- Cross-functional perspectives

### Step 3: Identify Competence Gaps

**Gap Analysis Process:**

1. **Compare Required vs. Current:**
   - List required competencies
   - Assess current competence level
   - Identify gaps for each competency
   - Prioritize gaps by impact and urgency

2. **Gap Categories:**
   - **Critical Gaps:** Immediate impact on quality, safety, or compliance
   - **Important Gaps:** Affect efficiency and effectiveness
   - **Development Gaps:** Support career growth and future needs
   - **Emerging Gaps:** New technologies, regulations, or practices (ISO 9001:2026)

3. **Document Gaps:**
   - Individual development plans
   - Team competency profiles
   - Organizational competency dashboard
   - Succession planning gaps
   - Digital transformation gaps
   - Sustainability competency needs

### Step 4: Take Action to Acquire Competence

**Training and Development:**

**Formal Training:**
- Classroom training (internal or external)
- Online courses and e-learning
- Webinars and virtual training
- Technical certifications
- Degree programs
- Professional development courses
- Digital skills bootcamps (ISO 9001:2026)

**On-the-Job Development:**
- Structured on-the-job training (OJT)
- Job rotation and cross-training
- Stretch assignments
- Project participation
- Shadowing and observation
- Apprenticeship programs

**Coaching and Mentoring:**
- One-on-one coaching
- Mentorship programs
- Peer coaching
- Leadership coaching
- Technical mentoring
- Virtual coaching platforms

**Self-Directed Learning:**
- Reading and research
- Online resources and tutorials
- Professional journals and publications
- Industry conferences and seminars
- Professional networking
- Microlearning and mobile learning

**Other Actions:**
- Hire competent personnel
- Contract with external experts
- Outsource specific activities
- Engage consultants
- Form partnerships with educational institutions
- Join industry associations and networks

### Step 5: Evaluate Effectiveness

**Evaluation Methods:**

**1. Reaction (Satisfaction):**
- Training evaluation surveys
- Participant feedback
- Relevance assessment
- Engagement measurement
- Net Promoter Score for training

**2. Learning (Knowledge Acquisition):**
- Pre and post-tests
- Knowledge assessments
- Skill demonstrations
- Certification achievement
- Competency re-assessment

**3. Behavior (Application):**
- On-the-job observation
- Performance monitoring
- Manager feedback
- Peer assessment
- Work quality metrics

**4. Results (Business Impact):**
- Quality metrics improvement
- Productivity increases
- Error reduction
- Customer satisfaction improvement
- Cost savings
- Process efficiency gains
- Innovation outcomes

**Evaluation Timeline:**
- **Immediate:** Reaction and satisfaction (end of training)
- **Short-term:** Learning and knowledge (within 1 week)
- **Medium-term:** Behavior and application (1-3 months)
- **Long-term:** Results and business impact (3-12 months)

### Step 6: Retain Evidence

**Documentation Requirements:**

**Individual Records:**
- Personal competency profile
- Education certificates and transcripts
- Training records and certificates
- Assessment results
- Performance appraisals
- Certifications and licenses
- Continuing education credits
- Digital badges and credentials (ISO 9001:2026)

**Organizational Records:**
- Competency matrix by role
- Training attendance records
- Training effectiveness evaluations
- Competency gap analyses
- Individual development plans
- Succession plans
- Training calendar and schedules

**Record Management:**
- Secure storage (digital and/or physical)
- Access control and confidentiality
- Retention period (typically employment + 3-7 years)
- Regular updates and reviews
- Backup and disaster recovery
- Integration with HR systems
- Cloud-based learning management systems

## Best Practices

### Competency Management Excellence

**1. Align with Strategy:**
- Link competencies to organizational goals
- Anticipate future competency needs
- Support digital transformation
- Enable sustainability objectives

**2. Make it Systematic:**
- Regular competency assessments
- Structured development planning
- Consistent evaluation methods
- Documented processes and records

**3. Engage Employees:**
- Involve employees in gap identification
- Co-create development plans
- Provide career development paths
- Recognize competency achievements

**4. Leverage Technology:**
- Learning management systems (LMS)
- Competency tracking software
- Online learning platforms
- Digital badges and credentials
- Mobile learning apps
- AI-powered learning recommendations

**5. Measure and Improve:**
- Track competency development metrics
- Evaluate training effectiveness
- Calculate training ROI
- Continuously improve programs
- Share success stories

**6. Support Continuous Learning:**
- Create learning culture
- Provide learning resources
- Allow time for development
- Encourage knowledge sharing
- Support professional development
- Embrace microlearning and just-in-time training

## Common Challenges and Solutions

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| Rapid technology change | Agile learning approach; continuous upskilling; partnerships with tech companies |
| Limited training budget | Leverage free online resources; peer learning; cross-training; microlearning |
| Time constraints | Integrate learning with work; short training modules; mobile learning |
| Measuring effectiveness | Clear success criteria; multiple evaluation methods; track business impact |
| Knowledge retention | Spaced repetition; practical application; ongoing refreshers; mentoring |
| Resistance to training | Demonstrate value; involve in planning; recognize achievements; gamification |
| Skill obsolescence | Continuous learning culture; technology monitoring; regular competency reviews |
