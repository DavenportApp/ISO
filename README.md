# ISO 9001:2026 Quality Management System Documentation

## Overview

This repository contains the complete Quality Management System (QMS) documentation compliant with **ISO 9001:2026** requirements. The documentation provides a comprehensive framework for achieving and maintaining quality management system certification.

### About ISO 9001:2026

ISO 9001:2026 is the latest version of the international standard for quality management systems. It specifies requirements for organizations that need to demonstrate their ability to consistently provide products and services that meet customer and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements.

**Key enhancements in ISO 9001:2026:**
- Enhanced quality culture and leadership requirements
- Climate change considerations integrated into QMS
- Expanded risk and opportunity management
- Digital transformation alignment
- Sustainability practices integration

## QMS Structure

Our Quality Management System follows the ISO 9001:2026 structure (Clauses 4-10):

### [Clause 4: Context of the Organization](./clause-4-context/)
Understanding internal and external issues, interested parties, QMS scope, and process approach.

### [Clause 5: Leadership](./clause-5-leadership/)
Leadership commitment, quality policy, and organizational roles and responsibilities.

### [Clause 6: Planning](./clause-6-planning/)
Risk and opportunity management, quality objectives, and planning of changes.

### [Clause 7: Support](./clause-7-support/)
Resources, competence, awareness, communication, and documented information.

### [Clause 8: Operation](./clause-8-operation/)
Operational planning, product and service requirements, design, production, and delivery.

### [Clause 9: Performance Evaluation](./clause-9-performance-evaluation/)
Monitoring, measurement, internal audit, and management review.

### [Clause 10: Improvement](./clause-10-improvement/)
Nonconformity, corrective action, and continual improvement.

## Quick Start Guide

1. **Review the Context** - Start with [Clause 4](./clause-4-context/) to understand organizational context
2. **Understand Leadership Requirements** - Review [Clause 5](./clause-5-leadership/) for leadership commitment
3. **Plan Your QMS** - Use [Clause 6](./clause-6-planning/) for risk-based planning
4. **Implement Support Processes** - Follow [Clause 7](./clause-7-support/) for resources and communication
5. **Execute Operations** - Implement [Clause 8](./clause-8-operation/) for product/service realization
6. **Measure Performance** - Use [Clause 9](./clause-9-performance-evaluation/) for monitoring and auditing
7. **Drive Improvement** - Apply [Clause 10](./clause-10-improvement/) for continual improvement
