## Purpose

This procedure establishes the requirements for management review to ensure the QMS:
- Remains suitable, adequate, effective, and aligned with strategic direction
- Delivers intended results
- Achieves customer satisfaction
- Responds to changing contexts and risks
- Drives continual improvement
- Allocates resources appropriately

**ISO 9001:2026 Alignment**: Enhanced emphasis on digital performance monitoring, climate and sustainability metrics, stakeholder value, and strategic agility.

---

## Scope

This procedure applies to:
- **Frequency**: Regular planned intervals (minimum annually, typically quarterly)
- **Participants**: Top management, process owners, management representative, quality manager, relevant stakeholders
- **Coverage**: Entire QMS including all processes, objectives, and performance
- **Organizational Levels**: Strategic, operational, and tactical

---

## Definitions

### Management Review
Strategic-level meeting where top management evaluates the QMS performance, suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness.

### Top Management
Person or group of people who direct and control the organization at the highest level (CEO, President, Board, Executive Team).

### Suitability
The QMS is appropriate for the organization's purpose, strategic direction, and context.

### Adequacy
The QMS is sufficient to meet requirements and achieve intended results.

### Effectiveness
The QMS achieves planned results and objectives.

### Alignment
The QMS supports the organization's strategic direction and business goals.

### Management Review Input
Information provided for consideration during the review.

### Management Review Output
Decisions and actions resulting from the review.

### Action Item
Specific task assigned with owner and due date resulting from management review.

## Responsibilities

### Top Management (President/CEO/Board)
- **Attend** management review meetings
- **Evaluate** QMS performance and effectiveness
- **Make decisions** on strategic direction, resources, changes
- **Ensure** review outcomes are implemented
- **Demonstrate** leadership and commitment to QMS

### Management Representative / Quality Manager
- **Schedule** and facilitate management review meetings
- **Prepare** review input materials (data pack)
- **Distribute** agenda and materials in advance
- **Record** meeting minutes and decisions
- **Track** action items and follow-up
- **Report** on previous action completion
- **Coordinate** input from process owners

### Process Owners
- **Provide** data and reports on their processes
- **Present** performance, issues, and opportunities
- **Participate** in reviews as requested
- **Implement** assigned actions

### Quality Team
- **Compile** performance data and metrics
- **Analyze** trends and patterns
- **Prepare** presentations and reports
- **Support** Management Representative

## Review Planning and Frequency

### Review Frequency

**ISO 9001:2026 Requirement (9.3.1)**: "At planned intervals" - minimum annually, but more frequent is recommended.

**Recommended Schedule**:

| Review Type | Frequency | Duration | Participants |
|-------------|-----------|----------|--------------|
| **Comprehensive Annual Review** | Once per year | Full day (4-6 hours) | Full executive team + process owners |
| **Quarterly Management Review** | Every 3 months | 2-3 hours | Executive team + key process owners |
| **Monthly Performance Review** | Monthly | 1 hour | Management Rep + Quality Manager + select owners |
| **Ad-hoc Review** | As needed | Variable | Relevant stakeholders |

**Factors Influencing Frequency**:
- Organization size and complexity
- Rate of change (market, technology, regulations)
- QMS maturity (new QMS = more frequent)
- Performance trends (issues = more frequent)
- Certification audit cycle
- Stakeholder expectations

### Annual Review Schedule Template

**Example Quarterly Schedule**:

| Quarter | Month | Focus Areas | Timing |
|---------|-------|-------------|--------|
| **Q1** | January | Year-end review, strategic planning, goal setting | Mid-quarter |
| **Q2** | April | Risk review, progress on initiatives, resource needs | Mid-quarter |
| **Q3** | July | Mid-year assessment, corrective actions, market changes | Mid-quarter |
| **Q4** | October | Performance against objectives, audit prep, budget planning | Mid-quarter |

**Scheduling Considerations**:
- Avoid peak operational periods
- Schedule before certification audits (review audit readiness)
- Allow time after quarter-end for data compilation
- Coordinate with financial reporting cycles
- Consider vacation/holiday periods

### Planning Activities

**4 Weeks Before Review**:
- Confirm date, time, location (or virtual platform)
- Send calendar invitations
- Assign input preparation responsibilities

**2 Weeks Before Review**:
- Compile data and prepare review pack
- Distribute draft agenda
- Request additional input if needed

**1 Week Before Review**:
- Finalize and distribute review pack
- Send reminder
- Confirm attendance

**Day Before Review**:
- Set up meeting room / test technology
- Print materials if needed
- Final reminder

## Review Inputs

**What to Include**:
- List of action items from last review
- Status of each (complete, in progress, delayed, not started)

- Effectiveness of completed actions

**Example Format**:

| MR-2024-Q4-01 | Implement new CRM system | IT Manager | Mar 2025 | 80% Complete | On track for completion |
| MR-2024-Q4-02 | Hire 2 quality engineers | HR/Quality | Feb 2025 | Complete | Both positions filled |
| MR-2024-Q4-03 | Revise training program | Training Lead | Jan 2025 | Overdue | Delayed due to resource constraints; new target: Mar 2025 |

#### 2. Changes in External and Internal Issues (Clause 4.1)

**External Issues** (PESTLE Framework):
- **Political**: Regulatory changes, government policies, trade agreements
- **Economic**: Market conditions, inflation, exchange rates, economic forecasts
- **Social**: Customer preferences, demographics, social trends
- **Technological**: New technologies, digitalization trends, automation
- **Legal**: New laws, compliance requirements, litigation risks
- **Environmental**: Climate change impacts, sustainability expectations, resource availability

**Internal Issues**:
- Organizational structure changes
- Strategic direction changes
- Resource availability (people, infrastructure, technology)
- Knowledge and competencies
- Organizational culture
- Performance trends

**Example**:

### Meeting Logistics

**Typical Duration**:
- **Comprehensive Annual**: 4-6 hours (may be split over 2 sessions)
- **Quarterly**: 2-3 hours
- **Monthly**: 1 hour

**Format Options**:
- **In-Person**: Preferred for annual comprehensive reviews
- **Virtual**: Acceptable, especially for quarterly reviews; use video conferencing
- **Hybrid**: Combination for distributed teams

**Materials Distribution**:
- Distribute data pack 1 week in advance
- Expectation: Participants review materials beforehand
- Meeting focuses on discussion and decisions, not data presentation

**Facilitation Tips**:
- Start and end on time
- Stick to agenda
- Focus on strategic discussions, not operational details
- Encourage participation from all attendees
- Use visual dashboards and charts
- Document decisions in real-time
- Park off-topic items in "parking lot" for later

### Progress Monitoring

**Management Representative Responsibilities**:
- Track action item status monthly
- Request updates from owners
- Identify roadblocks and escalate
- Provide status reports to top management
- Ensure actions stay on track

**Status Updates**:
- **Green**: On track, no issues
- **Yellow**: At risk, intervention needed
- **Red**: Overdue or blocked, urgent attention required

### Minutes and Documentation

**Meeting Minutes Contents**:
1. **Header**: Date, time, location, attendees
2. **Previous Actions Review**: Status of prior actions
3. **Inputs Reviewed**: Summary of data and information reviewed
4. **Key Discussions**: Major topics discussed (not verbatim)
5. **Decisions Made**: Clear decisions and rationale
6. **Actions Assigned**: Action register
7. **Next Meeting**: Date and focus

**Minutes Distribution**:
- Draft within 3 business days
- Review by top management
- Finalize within 5 business days
- Distribute to participants and relevant stakeholders
- Store in document management system

**Records Retention**: Minimum 5 years (or per regulatory requirements)
