# Climate Change Impact Assessment and Management

## ISO 9001:2026 Requirement

ISO 9001:2026 explicitly requires organizations to consider climate change as part of understanding the organization and its context (Clause 4.1) and in risk and opportunity management (Clause 6.1). Organizations must:
- Identify climate-related risks and opportunities
- Assess their impact on the QMS
- Plan and implement actions to address them
- Monitor and review climate impacts

## Purpose

This document provides the framework for integrating climate change considerations into the Quality Management System, ensuring organizational resilience and contributing to climate action.

## Climate Change Context

### Physical Climate Risks

**Acute Risks (Event-driven):**
- Extreme weather events (hurricanes, floods, wildfires)
- Temperature extremes (heat waves, cold snaps)
- Severe storms and precipitation
- Sea-level rise impacts
- Drought conditions

**Chronic Risks (Long-term shifts):**
- Rising average temperatures
- Changing precipitation patterns
- Increased weather variability
- Resource scarcity (water, materials)
- Ecosystem changes

### Transition Risks

**Policy and Legal:**
- Carbon pricing mechanisms
- Emission regulations
- Climate disclosure requirements
- Green building codes
- Environmental compliance standards

**Technology:**
- Shift to low-emission technology
- Automation and efficiency requirements
- Renewable energy adoption
- Electric vehicle transition
- Sustainable material alternatives

**Market:**
- Changing customer preferences
- Demand for sustainable products
- Green certification requirements
- Supply chain sustainability expectations
- Investor ESG criteria

**Reputation:**
- Stakeholder concerns about climate action
- Media scrutiny on environmental performance
- Employee expectations for climate leadership
- Community pressure for sustainability
- Brand value linked to climate action

## Climate Risk Assessment Process

### Step 1: Climate Exposure Identification

**Operational Exposure:**
- Facility locations and vulnerability assessment
- Supply chain exposure mapping
- Transportation and logistics routes
- Energy dependencies
- Water usage and availability
- Raw material sources

**Time Horizons:**
- Short-term (1-3 years)
- Medium-term (3-10 years)
- Long-term (10+ years)

### Step 2: Climate Risk Analysis

**Assessment Criteria:**
- **Likelihood:** Probability of climate event occurring
  - Rare (< 10%)
  - Unlikely (10-30%)
  - Possible (30-50%)
  - Likely (50-80%)
  - Almost Certain (> 80%)

- **Impact:** Consequence if event occurs
  - Insignificant (minimal disruption)
  - Minor (short-term disruption)
  - Moderate (significant but manageable)
  - Major (severe disruption)
  - Catastrophic (business continuity threat)

**Impact Categories:**
- Operational continuity
- Product/service quality
- Supply chain reliability
- Customer satisfaction
- Regulatory compliance
- Financial performance
- Employee safety
- Reputation

### Step 3: Climate Risk Prioritization

**Risk Matrix:**

| Likelihood/Impact | Insignificant | Minor | Moderate | Major | Catastrophic |
|------------------|---------------|-------|----------|-------|--------------|
| Almost Certain   | Medium        | High  | High     | Extreme | Extreme     |
| Likely           | Low           | Medium| High     | High    | Extreme     |
| Possible         | Low           | Medium| Medium   | High    | Extreme     |
| Unlikely         | Low           | Low   | Medium   | Medium  | High        |
| Rare             | Low           | Low   | Low      | Medium  | High        |

**Priority Actions:**
- Extreme: Immediate action required
- High: Action within 3 months
- Medium: Action within 6-12 months
- Low: Monitor and review

### Step 4: Climate Opportunity Identification

**Opportunity Categories:**

1. **Resource Efficiency**
   - Energy efficiency improvements
   - Water conservation
   - Waste reduction and circular economy
   - Material optimization
   - Process efficiency gains

2. **Products and Services**
   - Development of climate-friendly products
   - Low-carbon service offerings
   - Circular economy solutions
   - Green certifications
   - Innovation in sustainable materials

3. **Markets**
   - Access to green markets
   - New customer segments
   - Competitive differentiation
   - Premium pricing for sustainability
   - Partnership opportunities

4. **Resilience**
   - Enhanced business continuity
   - Diversified supply chains
   - Reduced operational risks
   - Improved stakeholder confidence
   - Regulatory compliance advantages

5. **Reputation**
   - Enhanced brand value
   - Improved stakeholder relations
   - Employee attraction and retention
   - Investor confidence
   - Industry leadership

## Climate Action Plan

### Mitigation Actions (Reducing Emissions)

**Energy Management:**
- Conduct energy audits
- Implement energy efficiency measures
- Transition to renewable energy sources
- Install energy monitoring systems
- Set carbon reduction targets

**Operational Improvements:**
- Optimize transportation and logistics
- Reduce business travel (leverage digital tools)
- Implement waste reduction programs
- Water conservation measures
- Sustainable procurement practices

**Product/Service Innovation:**
- Design for sustainability
- Life cycle assessment integration
- Circular economy principles
- Carbon-neutral product options
- Sustainable packaging solutions

### Adaptation Actions (Building Resilience)

**Infrastructure Resilience:**
- Facility climate vulnerability assessments
- Flood protection measures
- Temperature control systems
- Backup power systems
- Water management systems

**Operational Resilience:**
- Supply chain diversification
- Alternative supplier development
- Inventory buffering strategies
- Business continuity planning
- Emergency response procedures

**Workforce Adaptation:**
- Extreme weather protocols
- Remote work capabilities
- Health and safety measures
- Training on climate risks
- Communication systems

### Transformation Actions (Strategic Shifts)

**Business Model Innovation:**
- Service-based business models
- Sharing economy participation
- Circular economy integration
- Digital-first strategies
- Collaborative partnerships

**Strategic Planning:**
- Climate-aligned strategy development
- Long-term decarbonization roadmap
- Investment in low-carbon technology
- Market positioning for sustainability
- Stakeholder engagement on climate

## Climate Governance

### Roles and Responsibilities

**Top Management:**
- Overall accountability for climate strategy
- Resource allocation for climate action
- Review of climate performance
- Stakeholder engagement on climate
- Strategic decision making

**Sustainability Manager/Officer:**
- Climate risk assessment coordination
- Action plan development and implementation
- Performance monitoring and reporting
- Regulatory compliance management
- Stakeholder communication

**Process Owners:**
- Integration of climate considerations in processes
- Implementation of mitigation/adaptation actions
- Performance tracking in their areas
- Employee engagement and training
- Reporting to sustainability manager

**All Employees:**
- Awareness of climate impacts
- Participation in climate initiatives
- Reporting climate-related issues
- Contributing improvement ideas
- Sustainable practices in daily work

**Internal Reviews:**
- Monthly: Operational climate performance
- Quarterly: Management review input
- Semi-annual: Comprehensive risk assessment

**External Reporting:**
- Annual sustainability report
- Climate disclosure (TCFD, CDP, etc.)
- Regulatory submissions
- Stakeholder communications
- Certification documentation

## Climate Scenario Analysis

### Recommended Scenarios (IPCC-based)

**Scenario 1: Limited Climate Action (RCP 8.5)**
- Temperature increase: 3-5°C by 2100
- Severe physical risks
- Regulatory uncertainty
- Market disruption

**Scenario 2: Moderate Climate Action (RCP 4.5)**
- Temperature increase: 2-3°C by 2100
- Moderate physical and transition risks
- Evolving regulations
- Market transformation

**Scenario 3: Strong Climate Action (RCP 2.6)**
- Temperature increase: <2°C by 2100
- Significant transition risks
- Stringent regulations
- Rapid market shift to sustainability

**Analysis for Each Scenario:**
- Physical climate impacts on operations
- Regulatory and policy changes
- Market and customer behavior shifts
- Financial implications
- Strategic response requirements
