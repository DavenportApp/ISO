## Form Usage Guidelines

### When to Use Forms
- To capture required documented information
- To ensure consistency in data collection
- To facilitate process execution
- To maintain compliance records
- To support audits and reviews

### How to Complete Forms
1. Use current version of form
2. Complete all required fields
3. Obtain necessary approvals
4. Submit to appropriate authority
5. File in designated location
6. Maintain per retention schedule

### Form Management
- **Creation:** Forms created per document control procedure
- **Approval:** Forms approved by process owner and QMR
- **Distribution:** Forms available on intranet/shared drive
- **Revision:** Forms revised as needed, version controlled
- **Obsolete:** Old versions removed from circulation

## Digital Forms

## Form Retention

Forms (completed records) are retained per:
- Document Control Procedure
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Customer requirements
- Organizational policy
- ISO 9001:2026 requirements

Typical retention periods:
- Quality records: 5-10 years
- Audit records: 3-5 years
- Training records: Duration of employment + 3 years
- Customer complaints: 5 years
- Supplier evaluations: Life of relationship + 3 years

## Creating Custom Forms

To create a new form:
1. Identify need and justification
2. Draft form with necessary fields
3. Review with process owner
4. Approve per document control procedure
5. Add to forms directory
6. Train users on completion
7. Monitor effectiveness
